Reminders on your lock screen

iPhone only sends them to apps on your Home Screen. One-time setup:

  1. 1Open this page in Safari.
  2. 2Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3Open Thought Cards from your Home Screen.
  4. 4Go to Settings and tap Turn on under Notifications.

Thoughts saved here in the browser don’t move over to the Home Screen app. It starts fresh.

Privacy

Short and honest.

  • Your thoughts live on this device. They’re saved in this browser. There’s no account. Clearing your browser’s site data clears your Thought Bank too.
  • Lock screen reminders need a copy of your thoughts on your own reminder server, so it can send the nudge and the daily recap on time. Turning Notifications off removes that copy.
  • Microphone is only used while you’re recording, after your browser asks for permission. Your browser’s own speech service turns your voice into text.
  • Location is only checked when you tap “Use my current location”, after you allow it. Nothing is tracked in the background.
  • Reminders appear in the app while it’s open. With lock screen alerts on, they also arrive when the app is closed.
